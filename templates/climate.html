🌱 The Greenhouse Effect

How Greenhouse Gases Trap Heat

The greenhouse effect is natural and essential — without it Earth would be –18°C. The crisis is the enhanced greenhouse effect caused by burning fossil fuels, which has increased GHG concentrations far beyond Holocene norms.

⚠️ Climate Tipping Points

Points of No Return

Tipping points are thresholds beyond which a climate sub-system undergoes rapid, self-reinforcing, and largely irreversible change — independent of further human emissions. Science now identifies at least 16 such elements; five are at risk even at 1.5°C (Armstrong McKay et al., Science 2022).

⚠️ Critical finding: Tipping points can interact — cascading failures (Amazon collapse triggers Sahel drying; Arctic ice loss disrupts jet stream) may produce compound risks far exceeding individual assessments (Lenton et al., Nature Climate Change 2019).

🌍 Regional Impacts

No Region is Unaffected

💡 Solutions & Pathways

The Road to Net Zero

The IPCC AR6 Synthesis Report confirms: limiting warming to 1.5°C is still physically possible and economically feasible — but requires "rapid, far-reaching and unprecedented changes in all aspects of society" beginning immediately. Here's what that looks like.

🛡️ Mitigation — Cutting Emissions

Mitigation means reducing or eliminating GHG emissions at source — the only strategy that actually limits warming.

📜 Paris Agreement

The World's Climate Contract

Adopted in December 2015 and in force since November 2016, the Paris Agreement is the landmark international treaty on climate change. It unites 196 parties under a common framework to limit global warming and adapt to its inevitable impacts.
