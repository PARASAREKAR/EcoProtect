Agriculture & Irrigation

Growing crops and livestock requires massive volumes of water. Traditional flood irrigation loses up to 50% through evaporation and unlined canals. Meat and dairy production require 15,400 litres per kg of beef.

Restoration & Tech

Regenerative Water Systems & Sponge Cities

Working with natural hydrology rather than fighting it through concrete channeling.

🏙️

Sponge City Urban Design

Replacing impervious asphalt with permeable pavements, bioswales, sunken parks, and rooftop gardens that absorb 70–85% of urban stormwater, preventing floods and recharging groundwater.

🌿

Constructed Wetlands

Engineered reed beds and floating aquatic plants naturally filter wastewater, breaking down pathogenic bacteria, nitrogen, and heavy metals at 10% the operating cost of chemical treatment plants.

🌧️

Decentralized Rainwater Harvesting

Mandatory rooftop catchment systems channel clean rainwater into underground storage cisterns for toilet flushing, washing, and aquifer recharge shafts, providing resilience against dry spells.

💧

Solar Reverse Osmosis Desalination

Next-generation graphene membranes and photovoltaic-powered desalination provide clean coastal potable water without burning fossil fuels, coupled with mineral extraction from byproduct brine.
