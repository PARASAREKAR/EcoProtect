Generation Technologies

The Clean Power Spectrum

A complementary mix of zero-emission generation ensures grid stability 24/7/365.

☀️

Solar Photovoltaics (PV)

Harnesses sunlight via silicon semiconductors. Solar is now the cheapest form of electricity in history across most world regions. Rooftop and floating solar (floatovoltaics) avoid land competition.

Cost Decline (2010–2023): -89% (IRENA)
💨

Wind Energy (Onshore & Offshore)

Modern offshore turbines reach 260m height with 15 MW capacities, tapping consistent maritime winds. Wind delivers strong output during winter nights when solar is offline.

Capacity Factor: 45–60% (Offshore)
🌋

Next-Gen Geothermal

Enhanced Geothermal Systems (EGS) drill deep into hot crystalline rock to circulate fluid, producing completely baseload, weather-independent clean thermal energy with a near-zero land footprint.

Availability: 24/7/365 Continuous
🔋

Battery Energy Storage (BESS)

Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) and sodium-ion utility batteries store daytime surplus power for evening peak demand, delivering sub-second response times that out-perform peaker fossil plants.

Round-Trip Efficiency: 85–92%
💧

Green Hydrogen & Fuel Cells

Electrolyzers split water into pure hydrogen using renewable energy, providing clean high-heat fuel for steelmaking, chemical manufacturing, and maritime shipping where batteries are too heavy.

Target Cost by 2030: < $2.00 / kg H₂
🌊

Tidal & Pumped Hydro

Pumped storage hydropower acts as Earth’s giant gravity battery, representing over 90% of global grid storage. Marine tidal lagoons offer 100% predictable kinetic lunar generation.

Global Grid Storage Share: > 90% Worldwide

Global Decarbonization

Transforming the Four Heavy Emission Sectors

Clean electricity enables rapid decarbonization when we electrify everything and replace fossil fuels with green electrons.

🔌

1. Power Generation

Replacing coal and gas turbines with solar, wind, and storage. Smart grid interconnectors balance regional variations across continents via High-Voltage DC.

Potential: 100% Zero-Carbon Grid
🚗

2. Transportation

Battery electric cars, buses, and delivery vans deliver 3–4× higher thermodynamic efficiency than internal combustion engines. High-speed rail replaces regional aviation.

Cuts 24% of Global CO₂
🏡

3. Heating & Buildings

Heat pumps extract ambient thermal energy from outside air or ground, generating 300–400% efficiency. Magnetic induction hobs eliminate indoor nitrogen dioxide fumes.

300%+ Energy Efficiency
🏗️

4. Heavy Industry

Green steel using direct reduced iron (DRI) with green hydrogen removes coal coking. Electric arc furnaces and thermal battery storage provide 1000°C+ industrial process heat.

Decarbonizes Steel & Cement
