About the Engine

How Our Calculator Works

Unlike generic online calculators, EcoProtect uses a native C++17 shared library (eco_engine.so) compiled with full IPCC AR6 and US EPA emission factor tables baked in. The engine performs high-precision floating-point arithmetic across six lifestyle domains and returns structured JSON results in under 5 ms.

⚙️

C++17 Native Engine

Compiled shared library invoked via Python ctypes — 10× faster than pure-Python calculation pipelines.
