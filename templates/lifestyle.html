🚆

Switch to Rail

A London–Edinburgh rail journey emits ~22 kg CO₂ vs. ~150 kg by plane. Rail electrification continues to drop this further as grids decarbonise.

💚 Saves: up to 130 kg CO₂ per trip
🚗⚡

Electric Vehicles

EV benefits depend heavily on the electricity grid. In Norway (97% renewables), an EV emits ~7 g/km. In India (~70% coal), ~85 g/km — still better than petrol but context matters.

💚 UK: ~45 g/km vs. petrol 192 g/km
🚲

Cycle More

Replacing a 5 km car commute with cycling 5 days a week saves ~310 kg CO₂ per year, reduces congestion, improves air quality, and boosts physical health.

💚 Saves: ~310 kg CO₂/year
🚗👥

Carpool & Car-Share

Sharing a car journey with just one other person halves the per-person emissions. Apps like BlaBlaCar or Waze Carpool make this easy for commuters and long trips alike.

💚 Saves: up to 96 kg CO₂/km shared

🥗 Plant-Based 101

You don't need to go fully vegan. Reducing beef & lamb — the two highest-emission foods — has the greatest impact. Try: lentil bolognese, chickpea curries, tofu stir-fries, black bean tacos.

Protein sources: Lentils, chickpeas, tofu, tempeh, edamame, quinoa, hemp seeds, nuts.

🗑️ Food Waste Is a Climate Crisis

1/3 of all food produced globally is wasted, generating 8–10% of global GHG emissions — more than aviation (UNEP, 2021). Plan meals, use leftovers creatively, compost scraps. If food waste were a country, it would be the 3rd largest emitter.

🌾 Seasonal & Local Sourcing

Buying local seasonal produce reduces 'food miles' transport emissions and supports local farmers. Visit farmers' markets, join a community-supported agriculture (CSA) scheme, or grow your own herbs and salad leaves.

💡 Quick Win: One meat-free Monday per week saves ~430 kg CO₂ annually — equivalent to driving 1,700 km.

🔄 Alternatives to Ownership

🏛️
Library of Things

Borrow drills, camping gear, party equipment — tools you'd use once a year don't need to live in your home.

☕
Product-as-a-Service

Companies like Philips sell lighting-as-a-service; you pay per lux, not per bulb. This incentivises durability.

🔧
Repair Cafes

Free community workshops where volunteers help you fix electronics, clothing, furniture — extending product life dramatically.

🧊
Minimalism & Decluttering

Owning fewer things means less embodied carbon, less cleaning, less waste, and — research confirms — greater wellbeing.

🛒 Before You Buy — 5 Questions

  1. Do I actually need this, or just want it?
  2. Can I borrow or rent it instead?
  3. Can I buy it secondhand?
  4. Will I use it more than 30 times?
  5. Is it repairable when it breaks?
