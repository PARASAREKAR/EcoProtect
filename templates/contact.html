Tech Stack

About This Project

EcoProtect is an open-source environmental education platform built as an academic project. It combines a Python Flask web framework with a compiled C++17 calculation engine for scientific accuracy, all wrapped in a modern, accessible HTML5/CSS3/JavaScript frontend.

Backend: Python 3.11 · Flask 3.0 · SQLite (via SQLAlchemy)
Engine: C++17 shared library (eco_engine.so) via ctypes
Frontend: HTML5 · CSS3 (custom design system) · Vanilla JS ES2022
Templates: Jinja2 inheritance · responsive CSS Grid & Flexbox
Deployment: Gunicorn · Nginx reverse proxy · Linux (Debian 12)
Source Code: github.com/ecoprotect/ecoprotect-web

Frequently Asked Questions

🔬 Is the environmental data on EcoProtect scientifically accurate? ▼

Yes. All statistics, emission factors and ecological data used on EcoProtect are sourced directly from globally recognised scientific institutions including the IPCC Sixth Assessment Report (AR6), US EPA eGRID 2023, IEA World Energy Outlook, WHO Air Quality Guidelines (2021), WWF Living Planet Report, IUCN Red List and NOAA climate databases. Each data point includes its source reference. The carbon calculator uses the exact emission factors published in IPCC AR6 Working Group III, Chapter 2.

⚙️ How does the C++ calculator engine work? ▼

The carbon footprint calculator is powered by eco_engine — a native C++17 shared library compiled with emission factor tables baked in as static constexpr arrays. When you click "Calculate", the Python Flask backend invokes the library via Python's ctypes FFI, passing your inputs as a struct. The C++ engine performs multi-domain floating-point calculations and returns a JSON result within under 5ms. If the compiled library is not found (e.g. on Windows), the system seamlessly falls back to a pure-Python implementation that produces identical results.

📰 Can I contribute articles or research to the news section? ▼

Yes! You can submit article links and summaries directly from the News & Research page using the "Submit a Research Link" form. All submissions are reviewed by our editorial team for scientific credibility before being published. Submissions from peer-reviewed journals, major research institutions and established environmental organisations are prioritised. The admin panel can also be used to manage all submitted articles.

📅 How do I register for events and campaigns? ▼

Visit the Events page, browse available events using the filter bar (by category, location or keyword), and click the "Register" button on any event card. A registration modal will appear where you can enter your name, email, phone number and number of spots. After submitting, you will receive a confirmation email with event details. You can also propose your own campaign by contacting us via this page.

🌍 What is the One Planet Pledge campaign? ▼

The One Planet Pledge is EcoProtect's flagship community initiative, launched in September 2022. Over 12,000 people from 84 countries have signed a personalised commitment to adopt one or more eco-friendly habits — from planting trees and reducing plastic use to switching to public transport or solar energy. Signatories receive a digital certificate with a unique badge ID, access to the community leaderboard, and monthly impact updates. Visit the Pledge page to sign and get your certificate.

🔒 Is my personal data private and secure? ▼

Yes. EcoProtect is an educational project and does not sell, share or monetise user data. Information you provide (name, email, city) is stored securely in an SQLite database accessible only to the site administrator. Email addresses are used solely to send event confirmation and the monthly pledge newsletter. Calculator inputs are processed server-side and not stored — only your result is temporarily held in the session. You may request deletion of your data at any time by emailing team@ecoprotect.org.
