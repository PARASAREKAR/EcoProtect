🚀 Emerging Tech

Technology & Innovation on the Horizon

Six breakthrough technologies that could dramatically accelerate our path to net zero and ecological restoration.

⚗️

Green Hydrogen

SCALING UP

Electrolysis powered by renewables splits water into hydrogen — a clean fuel for steel, shipping, aviation & heavy industry that can't easily electrify.

Potential: Could decarbonise 15% of global emissions. Cost falling 50% by 2030. Leaders: H2 Green Steel, Air Products, Siemens Energy.
🌬️

Direct Air Capture

EARLY STAGE

Giant fans suck atmospheric CO₂ directly and store it underground or convert it to useful materials. Currently expensive ($300–1000/tonne) but cost curves are falling rapidly.

Potential: 1 billion tonnes CO₂/yr removal by 2050. Leaders: Climeworks, Carbon Engineering, 1PointFive (Oxy).
🏢

Vertical Farming

COMMERCIAL

Stacked indoor growing systems use 95% less water, zero pesticides, and can grow year-round near cities — reducing food miles and freeing agricultural land for rewilding.

Potential: $30B market by 2030. 390× more productive per land area. Leaders: AeroFarms, Bowery, Plenty, Infarm.
🍄

Mycelium Materials

GROWING FAST

Fungal root networks grown on agricultural waste create fully biodegradable alternatives to plastics, leather, and polystyrene packaging — storing carbon in the process.

Potential: Replace billions of tonnes of plastic annually. Leaders: Ecovative Design, Bolt Threads, Mogu.
🥩

Cultivated Meat

REGULATORY PHASE

Real meat grown directly from animal cells — no slaughter, 90% less land, 78% less greenhouse gas. Already approved in Singapore and USA. Cost falling from $300K to $10/kg.

Potential: Replace 30% of conventional meat by 2040. Leaders: Upside Foods, GOOD Meat, Mosa Meat.
🌊

Offshore Floating Solar

SCALING

Solar panels mounted on floating platforms on reservoirs, lakes and coastal waters — combining hydropower with solar, reducing evaporation by 70% and using no additional land.

Potential: 10 TW capacity on world's reservoirs alone. Leaders: Ciel & Terre, SolarDuck, BayWa r.e.
🏛️ Policy

The Global Policy Landscape

Major economies are legislating the transition — here's what the landmark policies mean and where they stand today.

🇪🇺

EU European Green Deal

World's most comprehensive climate package — targeting 55% emissions cut by 2030, carbon border adjustment, nature restoration law, and Fit for 55 legislative package. €1 trillion investment mobilised.

🟢 Active
🇺🇸

US Inflation Reduction Act (IRA) 2022

$369 billion in clean energy and climate investment — the largest climate legislation in US history. Tax credits for EVs, solar, heat pumps & clean manufacturing. Expected to cut US emissions 40% by 2030.

🟢 Law
🇮🇳

India's National Solar Mission

Target of 500 GW renewable capacity by 2030. India added 18.5 GW of solar in 2023 alone — the world's fastest growing solar market. PM-KUSUM for rural solar agriculture irrigation reaches 3.5M farmers.

🟡 On Track
🇨🇳

China National Carbon Market

World's largest emissions trading scheme, covering 2,200 power plants and 4.5 billion tonnes CO₂/yr — double the EU ETS. China has pledged peak emissions before 2030 and carbon neutrality by 2060.

🟡 Expanding
🇬🇧

UK Climate Change Act — Net Zero by 2050

First major economy to legislate net zero. UK has cut emissions 46% since 1990 — the fastest decarbonisation of any G7 nation. Offshore wind now powers 45% of electricity. North Sea Transition Deal accelerates clean-up.

🟢 Leading
