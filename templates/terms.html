📊 2. Scientific Data Integrity & Environmental Calculators

Our carbon footprint, water footprint, and forest sequestration computation engines utilize published scientific baselines provided by authoritative international bodies:

  • Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC): Sixth Assessment Report (AR6) global warming potentials and emissions factors.
  • National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA): Mauna Loa atmospheric carbon dioxide concentration benchmarks.
  • International Energy Agency (IEA): Renewable electricity grid penetration and lifecycle generation multipliers.
  • United Nations Food and Agriculture Organization (UN FAO): Global forest inventory and native species biomass sequestration rates.

While our algorithms and C++ embedded eco-engines provide high-precision estimations, personal lifestyle impacts may vary depending on micro-climate, local grid electricity fuel mixes, and individual consumption habits. Results are provided for environmental guidance and personal mitigation benchmarking.

🔐 3. User Accounts, Registration & Login Database Storage

When you create an account on EcoProtect to monitor your environmental control dashboard:

  • You agree to provide accurate, current, and complete registration information (including your name, email address, and optional city/focus area).
  • All login passwords are cryptographically hashed using industry-standard SHA-256 one-way hashing before storage in our SQLite relational database. Plaintext passwords are never logged, stored, or visible to platform operators.
  • You are responsible for safeguarding your credentials and for all actions taken under your authenticated profile.

💾 4. "Remember Me" Login Persistence & Cookie Architecture

To provide seamless environmental tracking across device restarts, EcoProtect offers a persistent "Remember My Login" feature:

  • When selected, a secure, cryptographically random 64-character session token is stored in the user_sessions table within the backend database, paired with an expiration timestamp (30 days from creation).
  • A corresponding secure HTTP cookie named eco_remember_token is placed on your browser, flagged with HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax protections to safeguard against cross-site scripting (XSS) and unauthorized token extraction.
  • You can terminate all active sessions and revoke remembered logins at any time by clicking Log Out or clearing your browser cookies.

🎛️ 5. Climate Control Scenarios & Mitigation Projections
