Credibility

Trusted Scientific Sources

All articles and data on EcoProtect are sourced from peer-reviewed research and globally recognised institutions.

🌍

IPCC

Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change — the UN's definitive climate science authority. Source for AR6 emission factors and warming projections.

🌊

NOAA

US National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration — ocean temperatures, CO₂ monitoring (Mauna Loa), Arctic sea-ice extent and hurricane data.

🐼

WWF

World Wide Fund for Nature — Living Planet Report (biennial biodiversity index), species conservation status and habitat protection data.

🦁

IUCN

International Union for Conservation of Nature — Red List of threatened species (42,000+ assessed), ecosystem vulnerability ratings and biodiversity metrics.

🇺🇳

UNEP

UN Environment Programme — Emissions Gap Report, Adaptation Gap Report, Global Resources Outlook and plastics pollution assessment data.

🔬

Nature Journal

World's most-cited peer-reviewed scientific journal — primary research on tipping points, permafrost thaw, coral bleaching and deforestation dynamics.

🏥

WHO

World Health Organisation — air quality guidelines, health impacts of climate change (250,000 extra deaths/yr by 2030), water safety benchmarks.

⚡

IEA

International Energy Agency — World Energy Outlook, per-country CO₂ emission inventories, renewable capacity statistics and net-zero pathway modelling.
